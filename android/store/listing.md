## en-US

**App name** (30): Tender for Frigate

**Short description** (80):
Watch, review and search your Frigate NVR cameras. Native, fast, no account.

**Full description**:

Tender is a native Android app for Frigate NVR, the open-source video recorder with object detection. It connects straight to your own Frigate servers: no account, no cloud, no tracking.

LIVE
• All your cameras at a glance, with live objects and a red outline while something moves
• Live video through go2rtc (MSE, WebRTC or MJPEG), sound and two-way talk
• Camera groups across servers, with Frigate's own icons, and "Now active" for the cameras where something happens
• Snapshots, manual recording, PTZ and pinch to zoom

REVIEW
• Alerts and detections from all your servers, with filters for camera, label, zone and date
• Mark items reviewed, one by one or all at once
• GenAI summaries and threat level when your server has them
• Motion: every camera at one moment, with a motion timeline

PLAYBACK
• Recordings per camera and day, as an event list or a zoomable timeline with a live preview while you scrub
• Save a clip to your phone, share it, or export it to Frigate

EXPLORE
• Tracked objects per label, semantic search, Find similar and license plates
• Face Library, custom classification models and exports with cases
• Ask Frigate's GenAI chat about your cameras

SETTINGS
• Several servers at once, with a remote address for when you are away
• Frigate's own settings: cameras, recording, detection, zones and masks, users, profiles, enrichments and more
• Viewers only see what Frigate allows them

Requires your own Frigate NVR server.

Tender is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Frigate project. Frigate is a trademark of Frigate, Inc. Tender is free software under the GNU GPL v3: https://github.com/sweebee/tender-for-frigate
